EMBER COURT

A duel of cards in a court of four elements. The Ashen Queen holds the throne — take it from her, one card at a time.

Goal: reduce the Queen to 0 life before she does the same to you.
Each round you gain energy (up to 6) and draw a card. Click a card to play it.
The court turns through Ember, Tide, Gale and Stone — cards of the court's element gain +2 power.
Resonance: play two cards of the same element in one turn and the second gains +1.
Block absorbs damage but fades at the end of your turn. Quake strikes through block.
Keys: Enter starts · Space ends your turn · M mutes · R restarts.

Original game · plays offline · no account needed
